# Central Library

## Library Timings

The central library is open from 8:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. on all working days of the
academic year. On Saturdays the library opens from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. The library is
closed on Sundays and on declared public holidays.

During the examination period the library extends its closing time to midnight.

## Borrowing Rules

Undergraduate students may borrow four books for a period of fourteen days. Postgraduate
students may borrow six books for twenty-eight days. Books must be returned or renewed
before the due date.

## Reference Section

The reference section holds dictionaries, encyclopedias and handbooks that may only be
consulted inside the library. Reference material cannot be borrowed or taken outside.

## Fines

Overdue books attract a fine of two currency units per day per book, capped at fifty
currency units per book. A book lost must be replaced or paid for at three times the
current list price.

## Study Space

The reading room seats four hundred students and is open on a first-come basis. Group
study rooms can be booked for one hour at the circulation desk. The discussion room
accommodates twelve people and can be booked for a maximum of two hours.
