# Academic Calendar

## Structure of the Semester

The academic year is divided into two semesters of sixteen teaching weeks each, followed
by one week of end-of-semester examinations. The teaching week comprises five working
days.

## Class Hours

The college working day is divided into two sessions. The morning session runs from 8:00
a.m. to 1:00 p.m. and the afternoon session from 2:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m. Each lecture is of
fifty minutes with a ten minute break between consecutive lectures.

## Public Holidays

The college observes the national holidays declared by the government, plus the founding
day of the institution. No classes are held on a declared holiday and attendance is not
marked.

## Project Submission

End-of-semester projects must be submitted to the department on the last working day of
the teaching calendar. A project submitted after the deadline is recorded as late and the
evaluation panel applies a penalty of ten percent of the awarded grade.
