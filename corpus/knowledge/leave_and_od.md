# Leave and On-Duty Requests

## Casual Leave

Students are entitled to twelve days of casual leave per semester. Casual leave is
requested through the student portal at least one working day in advance. Approval is
granted automatically unless the request overlaps an internal examination.

## Medical Leave

Medical leave is granted on production of a medical certificate issued by a registered
medical practitioner. Medical leave is not deducted from the casual leave quota. Requests
with a medical certificate are approved by the academic office within two working days.

## On-Duty (OD) Request

An on-duty request is used when a student represents the university in an event. An OD
request must be submitted through the student portal with a copy of the event invitation,
at least three working days before the event. The request must be countersigned by the
department faculty advisor and approved by the dean.

## Duty Leave for Sports

Students selected for university teams apply for duty leave through the physical education
department. Duty leave carries the same attendance exemption as on-duty leave and requires
proof of selection from the coach.

## Leave Cannot Be Backdated

Leave applications are not accepted for dates earlier than the date of application. A
student who omits an earlier absence must record it separately through the portal.
