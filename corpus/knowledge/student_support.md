# Student Support Services

## Academic Counselling

The academic counselling cell assists students who are struggling with a subject. A
student may book a counselling session through the student portal or walk in during the
published counselling hours. Sessions are confidential.

## Mentoring Programme

Every first-year student is allotted an academic mentor by the department. The mentor
meets the student once a fortnight during the first semester and once a month thereafter.

## Disability Support

The disability services office provides accessible study material, a scribe on request and
extra time during examinations. A student must submit the disability certificate at the
time of admission to avail of these facilities.

## Financial Assistance

Scholarships and fee concessions are administered by the scholarship section. Applications
open at the start of the academic year and close four weeks after admission. Incomplete
applications are not processed.
