# IT Services and Campus Facilities

## Student Portal

The student portal is the single channel for course registration, leave applications,
attendance marking, marks statements and fee payment. The portal is available twenty-four
hours a day except during scheduled maintenance on the last Sunday of each month.

## Wi-Fi Access

Campus wireless internet is available in the library, the academic block and the hostels.
Students authenticate with their registration number and the portal password. Wired
network points are provided in the reading room and the laboratories.

## Computer Laboratory Access

Laboratory access is restricted to registered laboratory courses. Students must carry their
identity card to the laboratory. Laboratory computers are configured with an installation
that is reverted nightly, so personal installations are not permitted.

## Transport

The college operates two buses on the campus loop, departing the main gate at 7:30 a.m.
and returning at 5:00 p.m. The hostel shuttle runs every twenty minutes between the hostel
block and the academic block.
