# Attendance Policy

## Minimum Attendance Requirement

Every registered student must maintain a minimum of 75 percent attendance in each
subject of study. Attendance is calculated per subject, not cumulatively across the
programme.

Students whose attendance in any subject falls below 75 percent are barred from writing
the end-of-semester examination in that subject.

## Condoned Leave

Students may apply for condoned leave through the academic office. Condoned leave is
granted only for medical reasons supported by a medical certificate, or for a genuine
emergency notified to the department within seven calendar days.

## Attendance Marking

Attendance is marked by the faculty member at the start of each lecture. A student who
arrives more than fifteen minutes late is marked absent for that lecture. Students must
register their attendance through the student portal within twenty-four hours.

## Short Attendance Consequences

A student with attendance between 70 and 75 percent receives a warning from the class
mentor. Two consecutive warnings result in a meeting with the academic counsellor. A
student who remains below 70 percent is removed from the course registration list for the
next semester.
