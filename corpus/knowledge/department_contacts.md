# Department Contact Information

## Department Offices

Each department maintains an office on the second floor of the academic block. Department
offices are open from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. on working days. The office is closed on
Saturdays and public holidays.

## Head of Department

The head of the department handles academic matters including course registration,
detention and endorsement of leave applications. Appointments with the head are scheduled
through the department office.

## Department Contact Numbers

The Computer Science department is reachable at extension 2101 and the Electronics
department at extension 2102. The examination cell is reachable at extension 2200 between
10:00 a.m. and 4:00 p.m.

## Administrative Office

The academic office issues bonafide certificates, conducts certificates and processes fee
payments. The office is located in the administration block and is open from 10:00 a.m. to
4:00 p.m.

## Laboratory Safety

Students working in a laboratory must read the safety instructions posted at the laboratory
entrance before beginning any experiment. A laboratory induction is mandatory before first
use of any equipment.
