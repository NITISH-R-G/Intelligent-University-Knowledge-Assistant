# Examination Rules

## Examination Pattern

Each subject carries one internal assessment at thirty percent and one end-of-semester
examination at seventy percent of the final grade. A student must score at least forty
percent in the internal assessment to be eligible to write the end-of-semester examination.

## Seating Arrangement

Seating is allocated by the examination cell and published on the student portal five days
before the examination. A student must occupy the assigned seat. Change of seat without
permission is treated as a misconduct under the examination code.

## Permitted Items

A candidate may carry a transparent pouch containing a pen, a pencil, an eraser and a
calculator approved by the department. Books, notes, mobile phones, smart watches and any
other electronic device are not permitted inside the examination hall.

## Invigilation and Reporting

Candidates must be seated fifteen minutes before the examination begins. A candidate who
arrives more than thirty minutes late is not admitted to the hall and is treated as having
absented the paper.

## Re-evaluation

A student may apply for re-evaluation of an answer script within seven days of the
publication of the result. The application must be submitted through the examination cell
with the prescribed fee.
